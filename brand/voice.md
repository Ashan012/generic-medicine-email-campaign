# Brand Voice Guide

## Our voice in three words
- helpful
- formal
- direct

## Words we use
- generic medicine
- same formula
- branded medicine
- sasti
- awareness
- aap

## Words we avoid
- cure
- treat
- prevent
- miracle
- guaranteed
- 100% safe
- ilaj
- beemar
- sale
- discount
- offer

## Claims we must never make
- We must never claim that a medicine can cure, treat, or prevent any disease.
- We must never say a generic medicine is "100% safe" or "guaranteed".
- We must never make any medical treatment claims.
- We must never frame this as a sale, discount, or offer campaign - it is an awareness campaign only.

## How we sign off
Generic Only
